About the Author

Posted by Pen<3 · Wife, mother, apparently a blogger now

Hi everyone! Welcome to my blog! In case you don't know me, I'm Penelope :) I'm a wife, mother, and apparently a blogger now. My husband Uly left a while back to explore the world and post about his adventures, so I thought I'd take a page out of his book and chronicle my day-to-day as well. Although, my life is probably much less interesting than his.

While he's out sailing around the world, I'm right here in Ithaca, keeping the lights on and raising our beautiful son Telemachus. So, if you want to hear me rant about my life, Uly's travels, and even our neighbor situation, then you've come to the right place. And in the case that you're Uly, answer your emails!!! I miss you!!!
