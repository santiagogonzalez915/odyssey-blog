About the Author

Posted by Pen<3 · Wife, mother, apparently a blogger now

Hi. I'm Penelope. Wife (technically), mother, homeowner, and, as of a few weeks ago, blogger. My husband Uly left a while back to chase his dream of becoming a travel influencer. Something about it being a "once in a lifetime opportunity." I'm still waiting to hear which lifetime, exactly, because it's been over a year and I'm still doing everything by myself.

In the meantime I'm here: keeping the lights on, raising our son Telemachus, managing some very persistent neighbors, and writing about all of it. Uly always said I should "put myself out there more." Well. Here I am, babe.

If you're reading this and you know where he is, please tell him to check his email.
